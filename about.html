About Us — Colorado Springs Mold Removal Specialists

Most homeowners dealing with mold in the Pikes Peak region have had the same experience: a contractor shows up, quotes a vague number, does something to the surface, and the mold comes back in four months. Springs Mold Solutions was built around a different approach — find the moisture source, contain the work area properly, remove what needs to go, and test to confirm the job is actually done. We're not the cheapest option in Colorado. We are the option that doesn't cut corners on containment or skip the clearance test to save time.

What We Do

We specialize in mold remediation and removal for homeowners and businesses throughout Colorado Springs, Fountain, Manitou Springs, Security-Widefield, Monument, and surrounding El Paso County communities. Our services include mold inspection and testing, full remediation with containment and HEPA air scrubbing, black mold removal, crawl space mold and encapsulation, water damage mold response, and commercial mold remediation.

What Makes Us Different

Written fixed-price quotes before any work starts. We inspect, determine scope, and give you an itemized written number — not a range, not "it depends." If we find something unexpected once walls are open, we stop and show you before any additional work proceeds. The price you're quoted is the price you pay for the agreed scope.

Clearance testing on every significant job. Most contractors consider the job done when their crew leaves. We recommend third-party air sampling after every remediation above a minimal scope — independent lab, not our own test. That test result is your proof the mold is gone, not our word for it. It also matters for insurance claims and future home sales.

We call back the same day. For active water damage situations, we respond within the hour. Mold growth begins within 24–48 hours of a water event — waiting until tomorrow isn't neutral, it's a choice that expands the problem and increases the final cost.

Who We Serve

We serve homeowners and businesses across Colorado Springs and the surrounding El Paso County area, including Fountain, Manitou Springs, Security-Widefield, Briargate, Old Colorado City, Monument, Woodland Park, Falcon, and Castle Rock. If you're outside our primary area, call us — we may still be able to help depending on scope.

Our Commitment

Every job gets a written quote before work starts. Every remediation includes proper containment — plastic sheeting and negative air pressure, not tape and a box fan. Every job ends with a clearance recommendation so you have documented proof the problem is resolved. If mold returns in a treated area within our warranty period due to factors within our scope of work, we come back at no charge.

Ready to Get Started?

The inspection is free and the estimate is written. Call us or schedule online — same-day response for water damage events.
